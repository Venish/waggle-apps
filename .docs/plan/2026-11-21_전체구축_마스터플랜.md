## 2. 물리 DB 60개 테이블 구축 계획 (`packages/db/src/schema/`)

PostgreSQL 18 (`drizzle-orm/pg-core`) · 단일 `public` 스키마 · `BIGINT GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY PRIMARY KEY` · `TIMESTAMPTZ` · `ON DELETE RESTRICT` · Partial Unique (`WHERE is_deleted = false`):

1. `01_auth.ts` (**7**): `accounts`, `users`, `devices`, `auth_sessions`, `admins`, `admin_sessions`, `admin_otp_codes`
2. `02_dog_daily.ts` (**7**): `dogs`, `breeds`, `anniversaries`, `daily_entries`, `daily_entry_stickers`, `common_codes`, `vaccination_records`
3. `03_onboarding.ts` (**8**): `survey_questions`, `survey_answers`, `survey_responses`, `tags`, `course_tags`, `answer_tags`, `level_tests`, `level_test_attempts`*(APPEND_ONLY)*
4. `04_education.ts` (**10**): `courses`, `units`, `lessons`, `lesson_steps`, `practice_checklists`, `quizzes`, `quiz_questions`, `quiz_attempts`*(APPEND_ONLY)*, `enrollments`, `lesson_progresses`
5. `05_gamification.ts` (**3**): `daily_goals`, `badges`, `dog_badges`
6. `06_points.ts` (**4**): `point_wallets`, `point_ledgers`*(APPEND_ONLY)*, `payment_event_logs`*(APPEND_ONLY)*, `point_recharges`
7. `07_commerce.ts` (**11**): `product_categories`, `products`, `product_images`, `product_variants`, `carts`, `cart_items`, `addresses`, `orders`, `order_items`, `shipments`, `course_unlocks`
8. `08_trainer_qa.ts` (**4**): `trainers`, `trainer_tags`, `inquiries`, `trainer_settlements`
9. `09_notifications.ts` (**2**): `notification_templates`, `notifications`*(APPEND_ONLY)*
10. `10_operations.ts` (**4**): `audit_logs`*(APPEND_ONLY)*, `banners`, `policy_configs`, `policy_config_histories`*(APPEND_ONLY)*
